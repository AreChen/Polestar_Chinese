Polestar Chinese Community Fix (unofficial)
Release: 0.3.7.2-zh.1-navfix.1 (beta)

This is a community fork of Polestar by 0xFitz and the Polestar
contributors, with Chinese localization by ray929 and contributors.
Original project: https://github.com/fitzforge/polestar
Chinese version: https://github.com/ray929/Polestar_Chinese
This fork's source: https://github.com/AreChen/Polestar_Chinese

On 2026-11-16, AreChen re-encoded the generated navigation data using
equivalent Lua decimal escapes to prevent UTF-8 corruption, and changed
the main addon's title, author and website metadata to identify this fork.
The other addon folders are included from the Chinese release. Their
matching in-game TOC version remains 0.3.7.2 for compatibility checks.

The original license and attribution files are retained. This fork is

It is independent and is not endorsed by the original authors or Blizzard.
The beta has not yet been verified in the game client.
